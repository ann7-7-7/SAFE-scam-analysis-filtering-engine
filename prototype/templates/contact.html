FAQ

Can I paste a real suspicious message?

You can paste suspicious text for checking, but avoid including passwords, OTPs, bank details, private documents, or sensitive personal information.

Does S.A.F.E store my message?

No. S.A.F.E is designed to analyse messages temporarily and not store user-submitted message content by default.

Is S.A.F.E always correct?

No. S.A.F.E provides risk guidance only. Always verify suspicious messages through official channels.

Can I check WhatsApp, Telegram, SMS and email messages?

Yes. S.A.F.E is designed to help analyse suspicious messages from SMS/Text Message, WhatsApp, Telegram, Email, Social Media DMs and other sources.

Ready to check a suspicious message?

Open the S.A.F.E Checker and review the warning signs before clicking, replying, downloading files, or sending money.
