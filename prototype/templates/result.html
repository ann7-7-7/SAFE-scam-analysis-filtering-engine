{# —— What should I do now? —— #}

What should I do now?

Follow these steps in order. They are safe for any type of scam message.

①

Do not click links

Links can go to fake sites. If you need the service, open the official app or type the website yourself.

②

Do not send money

Scammers rush you to pay by transfer, gift cards, or crypto. Stop and check with someone you trust.

③

Do not share OTPs or passwords

Banks and real services never ask for your full password or codes by message.

④

Verify using an official source

Find the phone number or website on your bank card, bill, or search for the company’s official site—not from this message.

⑤

Block and report the sender

Use “Report spam” or “Block” in your app, and tell Action Fraud (UK) or your platform’s help centre if you lost money or data.
